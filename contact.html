Frequently Asked Questions

Some quick answers before you make your booking.

How do I book a photography session?

Fill in the booking form above and submit it. Your details will be prepared in WhatsApp so you can communicate directly with TJ Shuttervibes and discuss availability and requirements.

Can you photograph events outside Homa Bay?

Travel and location arrangements can be discussed before confirming the booking. Contact us with the event location, date and type of photography required.

What information should I provide when booking?

Include your name, phone number, type of shoot, preferred date, location, approximate number of people and any special photography requirements.

Can I request a customized photography package?

Yes. Share the type of occasion, coverage duration, location and photography expectations so the available options can be discussed with you.
